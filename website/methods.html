No need to choose alone

Describe the issue. Consensus can route it.

The built-in Method Recommender classifies the topic and desired answer, then suggests the best-fitting methods. For a collaborative choice, Guided Triage has the moderator ask about the problem, decision context, and uncertainty before the group confirms a recommendation and switches methods automatically.
